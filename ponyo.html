Ponyo

My second most watched Ghibli film

Summary:

Ponyo is a fish. She is the daughter of a sea sorcerer and goddess. Ponyo dreams of becoming a human, so one day she escapes the sea to the land. She is found by a boy named Sosuke, who lives in a house overlooking the sea, who takes her home and cares for her. The longer she's on land, however, the more she transforms into a human girl, to the dismay of her father. Ponyo's transformation also sets off a chain of natural disasters in the town, including a flood. Sosuke and Ponyo must work together to restore the balance of nature to save their town.
