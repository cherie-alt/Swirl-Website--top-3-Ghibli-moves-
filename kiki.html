Kiki's Delivery Service

This movie is very nostalgic for me: I've watched it at least 10 times.

Summary

Kiki is a 13 year old witch. In her family it is customary for witches to leave home at that age to make their own way in the world. Kiki sets off on her broom with Momo, her black cat, in search of a town to live in. Kiki settles down in a coastal town and opens a delivery service for a baker who gives her a place to stay. It's a beatiful coming of age story where Kiki gets help from many people as she tries to figure out who she is.
