When Marnie Was There

Summary:

"Anna, a lonely and asthmatic foster child, is sent to a remote Norfolk village for the summer to improve her health. She feels like an outsider until she finds the abandoned Marsh House and meets Marnie, a mysterious golden-haired girl her exact age. The two girls quickly form an intense, secret friendship, meeting for adventures and sharing their deepest thoughts, despite Marnie's strange appearances and disappearances. As their bond deepens, Anna feels a connection she has never known, but also senses that Marnie is tied to the house's past. Through cryptic clues, including Marnie's diary and a new family at the Marsh House, Anna slowly uncovers the truth about Marnie's identity and their unexpected relationship, finding a sense of belonging and understanding about her own family history."
